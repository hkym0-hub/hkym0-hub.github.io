About Me

Design

Hi! I'm Hur Kim Yumi, a student at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU) majoring in Film, TV and Multimedia.

My passion lives at the intersection of motion graphics and visual design — I love giving ideas a visual language that breathes and moves. Whether it's a title sequence, a brand animation, or a UI transition, I believe movement is the most powerful form of communication.

Currently exploring the creative possibilities of After Effects, Cinema 4D, and generative design tools to push what's possible in motion.
