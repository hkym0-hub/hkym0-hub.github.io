Who I Am

About Me

🎬
📍 Seoul, Korea

Hi! I'm Hur Kim Yumi, a creative student at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU) majoring in Film, TV and Multimedia. I'm passionate about visual storytelling and the art of bringing ideas to life on screen.

I explore the intersection of technology and narrative — from short film production to multimedia content creation. I believe every frame tells a story, and I strive to make each one count.

Currently expanding my skills in digital media production, video editing, and creative direction, with a deep interest in how emerging technologies are reshaping the world of film and television.
